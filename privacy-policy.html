Who we are

SBM ConTech Industries is a South African technology business. For privacy questions, contact sbmcontechindustries@gmail.com or +27 64 026 2150.

Information you share

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email, service interest and project message. A business name is optional. We use these details to respond, discuss your requirements, prepare a proposal and manage our relationship with you. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Enquiries and external services

Formspree processes form submissions. Email, WhatsApp and Instagram are separate services with their own privacy terms. Their providers may process information outside South Africa. We share enquiry information with the providers and team members who need it to handle your request, or where required by law. Read Formspree’s privacy policy, GitHub’s privacy statement and Cloudflare’s privacy policy for their processing practices.

LAEL assistant

LAEL’s local guide works in your browser. When the AI service is available, your current conversation is sent through SBM’s Cloudflare service to OpenAI to generate a reply. Provider processing follows their applicable terms. The website does not save chat history in browser storage. If you choose microphone input, your browser may use an external speech recognition service. Voice and microphone access are optional and off until you activate them.

Cookies and analytics

We store your cookie preference in your browser for up to six months. Optional analytics loads only if you allow it and a valid analytics property is configured. Analytics is currently inactive. If activated later, Google Analytics may process page views, device information and interactions such as enquiry completion. Enquiry text, names and email addresses are not sent by our analytics integration. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer. See our cookie notice.

Hosting and security

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Hosting and security providers may process technical request data, including IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the service. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee complete security.

Keeping your information

We keep enquiry and business records for as long as reasonably needed to respond, deliver agreed services, resolve issues or meet legal obligations. You may ask us about the records we hold and request deletion where applicable.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above; we may need to verify your identity. You can also raise a concern with South Africa’s Information Regulator.

Changes

We will update this page when our website’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current notice.
