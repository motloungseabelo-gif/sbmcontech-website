BEFORE WE BUILD

A clearer starting point.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Describe the problem, who uses the current process, and what success would look like. You do not need a technical specification. Our project scoping form helps turn that context into an initial brief.

How much will my project cost?

Pricing depends on the scope, integrations and support needs. Share your goals through the project scoping form so the SBM team can discuss a quote. The form’s budget ranges are for project context, rather than fixed package prices.

Can you improve an existing system?

Yes. Start with the workflow and the software you already use. SBM can help define improvements to interfaces, business processes and integrations, or scope a replacement where needed.

Can LAEL provide a quote or book a meeting?

LAEL explains SBM services and guides you to the right page. It cannot provide a binding quote, accept payments, submit your form or book a meeting. Use the contact page for commitments from the team.
