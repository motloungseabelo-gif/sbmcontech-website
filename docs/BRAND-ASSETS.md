# Brand and photo assets

The new custom mark is `images/redesign/sbm-mark.png`, with optimized 48, 96, 192 and 512 px WebP variants. The navigation uses the new emblem with a live-text wordmark; favicons, Apple touch icon, manifest icons and `favicon.ico` use the same emblem. The 1200 × 630 share image is `images/redesign/og-sbm.png`.

The emblem was created with the built-in image generation tool, then trimmed, resized and compressed for the website. Original generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: new custom production logo for SBM ConTech Industries, a South African software, AI automation and connected infrastructure company. Create one premium minimalist geometric emblem only, no lettering. A distinctive monogram suggesting a folded S built from three precisely interlocking broad architectural ribbon segments, with subtle circuit-connection logic and a single small square terminal. Flat vector-style, crisp consistent edges, perfectly balanced silhouette, extremely readable as a tiny favicon. Use deep navy #10243A and warm royal bronze brown #825238, with a small warm gold #C19A6B detail. Transparent background, generous even clear space around one centered compact mark, square composition. Sophisticated and human, appropriate for a calm warm-white editorial tech website. No gradients, no 3D, no mockup, no shadow, no extra symbols, no border, no text, no watermark. The icon must stand alone and remain legible at 24 pixels.

Photography is illustrative infrastructure imagery, not a claim that SBM owns the pictured facility or has delivered the pictured buildings.

- Server/network equipment: Taylor Vick on Unsplash; source image `photo-1558494949-ef010cbdcc31`, [photo page](https://unsplash.com/photos/M5tzZtFCOfs), [Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license).
- Johannesburg city: Magda Ehlers on Pexels, [photo 9890336](https://www.pexels.com/photo/aerial-view-of-city-buildings-9890336/), [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/).

The site serves local AVIF images with WebP fallbacks at 480, 800 and 1200 px. All images carry meaningful alternative text and explicit dimensions. Below-the-fold images load lazily. Source JPEGs are excluded from the published build.
