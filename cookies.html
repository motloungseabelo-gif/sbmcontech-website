Essential preferences

We use a browser storage entry called sbm-cookie-consent-v1 to remember your cookie choice for up to six months. The site may use a short-lived session entry to show enquiry confirmation. We do not store enquiry content in these entries.

Optional analytics

The GA4 integration is prepared, but analytics is currently inactive because no measurement property is configured. If configured later, it loads only after you allow analytics. Google Analytics may then set cookies such as _ga to measure website use. Our integration does not send names, emails or enquiry messages to analytics.

Changing your choice

You can allow or reject optional analytics below, or reopen settings from any page’s footer. Rejecting stops analytics; we remove accessible Google Analytics cookies and reload the page to stop loaded tracking code.
